    About the department

    Computer Applications

    60MCA seats · Master of Computer Applications
    60MCA seats · Integrated MCA
    12Faculty members
    2006Established

    Established in 2006, the Department of Computer Applications at the Federal Institute of Science and Technology (FISAT) offers a two-year MCA programme with an annual intake of 60 students, an Integrated MCA programme with an annual intake of 60 students, and Ph.D. programmes. Affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU) and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), these programmes aim to empower aspiring professionals with the knowledge, skills, and expertise needed to excel in the field of technology.

    The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) programme is a two-year postgraduate programme that focuses on the study of information technology and computer science. The Integrated MCA programme, introduced to cater to the evolving demands of the industry, is a five-year programme designed for students who have completed their higher secondary education. Covering a wide array of subjects—including programming languages, advanced data structures, databases, software engineering, web development, cloud computing, and artificial intelligence—both MCA programmes enable students to specialize in specific areas of computer science, enhancing their expertise and increasing employability in the dynamic IT industry.

    The department’s mission revolves around achieving academic excellence by providing quality education, enhancing students’ mastery skills, and offering professional training to ensure their readiness for successful careers, with a strong emphasis on social commitment. The department’s strategic planning at the outset of each academic year aims to position itself as a center of excellence in professional education while upholding a commitment to continuous quality assurance.

    Backed by a team of highly qualified and dedicated faculty members with substantial expertise, the department consistently secures top positions in University Examinations. The vibrant placement cell plays a pivotal role in facilitating excellent job opportunities in renowned MNCs, including IBM, TCS, G10X, IBS, CTS, SOTI, and leading banks like Federal Bank. To bridge the gap between industry and academia, the department organizes industry-readiness training programs, domain expert workshops, and extracurricular activities to enrich students’ knowledge and skills. Annual initiatives such as conferences, faculty development programmes, workshops, expert talks, project exhibitions, and personality development courses further contribute to the department’s reputation for excellence. With a strong emphasis on practical exposure, industry collaborations, and research-driven learning, both MCA programmes equip students with a competitive edge in the rapidly evolving tech landscape.

    Programmes

    MCA (Two Year)

    The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) program at FISAT is a prestigious and highly sought-after course designed to shape students into top-class computer professionals. The program is structured to provide a strong foundation in computing, software development, and emerging technologies while also emphasizing practical exposure and industry relevance.

    • Two year post-graduate programme consisting of four semesters.
    • The annual intake is 60.
    • The programme is approved by AICTE and affiliated to the A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technological University, Kerala.

    Key Features:

    • Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS)
    • Industry-Relevant Curriculum
    • Placement & Industry Connect:
    • Research & Innovation Opportunities:
    • Holistic Development

    Eligibility Criteria:

    • Passed BCA/ Bachelor Degree in Computer Science Engineering or equivalent Degree OR
      Passed B.Sc./ B.Com./ B.A. with Mathematics at 10+2 Level or at Graduation Level (with additional bridge Courses as per the norms of the concerned University).
    • Obtained at least 50% marks (45% marks in case of candidates belonging to reserved category) in the qualifying Examination
    • Must qualify entrance examination conducted by a competent authority as per the rules and regulations of KTU/AICTE/Govt
    Integrated MCA

    The Department of Computer Applications at Federal Institute of Science and Technology (FISAT), established in 2006, offers a transformative educational experience. The Integrated MCA (Master of Computer Applications) programme at FISAT is a five-year, industry-aligned program designed for students who want a comprehensive education in computing, software development, and emerging technologies.

    • Five-year Post-graduate Programme consisting of 10 Semesters.
    • The annual intake is 60.
    • The programme is approved by AICTE and is affiliated to the A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technological University, Kerala.

    Key Features:

    • Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS)
    • Industry-Relevant Curriculum
    • Placement & Industry Connect
    • Research & Innovation Opportunities:
    • Holistic Development

    Eligibility Criteria:

    • Passed 10+2 (SSLC and Plus 2) Examination. Obtained at least 45% marks (40% in the case of candidates belonging to reserved categories) in the qualifying Examination.

    Additionally, candidates who have not studied Mathematics at the higher secondary level will be required to complete a compulsory bridge course formulated by the university.

    Candidates awaiting results of the qualifying examinations are also eligible to apply for admission provided they shall produce the proof of qualification on or before the date of admission by the College

    Ph.D

    The Department of Computer Applications is a recognized Research Centre under APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU), offering opportunities to pursue Ph.D. programmes in Computer Science and related interdisciplinary areas.

    The department offers Full-Time and Part-Time Ph.D. programmes for aspiring researchers committed to advancing knowledge and developing innovative solutions to real-world challenges. Research scholars receive expert guidance and mentorship throughout their doctoral journey, supporting them in identifying research problems, conducting systematic investigations, publishing research findings, and disseminating their work through reputed journals and conferences.

    The Research Centre coordinates and monitors the progress of research scholars and facilitates a supportive environment for pursuing quality research.

    Facilities

    Laboratories

    The Federal Institute of Science and Technology (FISAT) has a well-developed Central Computing Facility (CCF) equipped with modern, high-performance computers and essential peripherals to support the academic, teaching, learning, and administrative computing requirements of students and faculty. Established in May 2009, the CCF comprises nine well-equipped computer laboratories with an adequate number of computers and supporting infrastructure. The facility provides a reliable and conducive environment for practical sessions, programming, project work, research, and other technology-enabled academic activities. For the Department of Computer Applications, the CCF laboratories are allocated as follows:

    Lab No. Laboratory Name Allocation
    L4 Project Lab Department of Computer Applications
    L6 Design Project Lab Department of Computer Applications
    L9 (Shared) Programming Lab Shared facility

    The laboratories are utilized through a structured, timetable-based allocation to ensure adequate access for all practical courses and academic activities. Courses involving practical components are provided with scheduled laboratory sessions every week as per the academic timetable. Each laboratory is placed under the charge of one teaching faculty member and two laboratory instructors, ensuring effective academic and technical support. The laboratories are equipped with sufficient hardware and relevant software to support programming, practical sessions, project work, and other academic activities. Free and open-source software is utilized wherever appropriate to promote hands-on learning and support the academic requirements of the programme.

    Scope of employment

    • Graduates can pursue diverse career roles such as Software Developer, Full Stack Developer, AI/ML Engineer, Data Scientist, Data Analyst, Cloud Engineer, Cybersecurity Analyst, Database Administrator, Web/Mobile Application Developer, System Analyst, and Technical Consultant.
    • The programmes prepare graduates for opportunities in emerging technology domains such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Cloud Computing, IoT, Software Engineering, Web and Mobile Application Development, Database Management, and Big Data Analytics.
    • Career opportunities are available across IT service and product-based companies, startups, e-commerce, banking and fintech, healthcare, education, government organizations, and public sector undertakings (PSUs).
    • The programmes provide a strong foundation for higher studies and research, including specialized postgraduate programmes, PhD and R&D careers in computing and related disciplines.
    • Graduates can also pursue academic and teaching careers in higher education institutions, subject to the prescribed qualifications and eligibility criteria.

    Achievements, MoUs & more

    Higher Studies

    The Department of Computer Applications encourages students to pursue higher education, research, and advanced learning opportunities in emerging areas of computer applications and information technology. The department supports students aspiring to pursue Ph.D. programmes, specialized certifications, and research opportunities. Through academic guidance, mentoring, and career counselling, students are encouraged to identify suitable pathways aligned with their academic interests and career aspirations. The Ph.D. programme provides an opportunity for aspiring researchers to undertake advanced research in emerging and relevant areas of computing and contribute to the advancement of knowledge.

    MoUs

    The Department of Computer Applications, FISAT, actively collaborates with industries and professional organizations to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements. These interactions provide students with opportunities for practical exposure, skill development, internships, training, expert interactions, and placements. Through MoUs, the department fosters sustained industry collaboration in technical training, workshops, industry visits, internships, placements, faculty development, and research.

    FM Code Solutions, an industry-supported Mini IT Company established on campus in association with Logix Space Technologies Pvt. Ltd., Kochi, was inaugurated on 14 June 2024. The initiative, based on the company’s Link Ur Codes product, bridges academia and industry by providing students with exposure to emerging technologies, soft skills, and real-world IT practices. Operating as a scaled-down IT company, FM Code Solutions provides students with opportunities to work on live projects and gain practical industry experience throughout their academic journey.

    Centre for Future Skills (CFS)

    Under the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 framework, the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC), under the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, Government of India, entrusted FISAT with establishing Kerala’s first Centre for Future Skills (CFS). Implemented by Ethnotech Academy, the centre offers industry-certified pathways in AI/ML, Cloud & DevOps, IoT, and Web Design, with support from global technology leaders such as AWS, Microsoft, Intel, IBM, and Apple, enhancing students’ industry readiness, practical skills, and employability.
