    About the department

    Civil Engineering

    120B.Tech seats · Civil Engineering
    24M.Tech seats · Structural Engineering & Construction Management
    26Faculty members
    2011Established

    The Department of Civil Engineering started offering a B.Tech programme in Civil Engineering in the year 2011 with an intake of 60, which was increased to 120 in the year 2013. The B.Tech programme is NBA accredited upto June 2027. M.Tech in Structural Engineering and Construction Management was added in the year 2014 with an intake of 24. A team of qualified faculty with ample experience is the highlight of the Department.

    The B.Tech graduate program in Civil Engineering envisages to provide students with a good understanding of Civil Engineering solutions in a global, societal and environmental context, consistent with the principles of sustainable development. The course will make the students capable in identifying, formulating and solving civil engineering problems that meet specified performance, cost, time, safety and other quality needs and objectives with professional and ethical responsibility.

    The M.Tech graduate program in Structural Engineering and Construction Management focuses to impart advanced knowledge in the field of Structural Engineering and Construction Management and make them capable of providing solutions with professional and ethical responsibility. The course also gives importance to research based projects and to prepare students for teaching and further research, in addition to making them ready to satisfy current needs of the industry.

    Both the programs emphasize on industry and research based projects, which would enhance their skills to become successful professionals for a holistic development.

    The department is actively involved in consultancy services in the area of Material Testing and Design.

    The Department of Civil Engineering was established in the year 2002. Initially, the department was working as a support for other engineering branches and offered courses like Basic Civil Engineering, Engineering Mechanics and Engineering Graphics for the first year students and Strength of Materials (Theory and Laboratory courses) for Mechanical Engineering students.

    Purpose

    Vision & Mission

    Vision

    Emerge as a globally recognized Center of Excellence in Civil Engineering by providing technical education, pioneering sustainable innovation and research and fostering ethical, socially responsible leadership.

    Mission

    Mission 1 (Technical Education): Deliver practical civil engineering education in design, planning, materials, analysis, tools, and maintenance.

    Mission 2 (Research & Innovation): Advance sustainable, resilient civil engineering innovation through interdisciplinary research addressing societal needs.

    Mission 3 (Ethics & Social Commitment): Cultivate ethically grounded civil engineers committed to leadership, community service, and sustainable infrastructure.

    MoUs

    MoU with Karthas Academy and Kochuthommen Associates

    A Memorandum of Understanding(MoU) is signed between Federal Institute of Science and Technology, Angamaly, Karthas Academy and Kochuthommen Associates, Ernakulam, on 21st January 2026 for offering Add-on Course on “Diploma in Interior Design” to the final year students. This collaboration reflects the continuous efforts of the Department to bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world professional requirements. The MoU aims to provide students with opportunities to gain practical exposure, skill-based training, and certification support in emerging and in-demand areas of Civil Engineering. Through this partnership, students will be able to access structured training modules, expert mentoring, and hands-on learning sessions designed to enhance their technical competencies and employability. This is the second edition of the course being conducted in the Department. In its first edition, 30 students were successfully placed in leading construction and interior design firms such as Asset Homes, Trine holdings, D’Life etc.

    MoU with University of Malaysia Al-Sultan Abdullah Pahang, Malaysia

    A Memorandum of Understanding(MoU) is signed between Federal Institute of Science and Technology, Angamaly and University of Malaysia Al-Sultan Abdullah, Pahang, Malaysia to encourage the following activities, in particular, to promote international academic and research cooperation in the areas of organization of joint symposia, online courses to students, Research collaboration in terms of joint grant application, and mutual involvement of human and technical resources, Institutional exchanges between students and staff from each partner institution. The staff members from the department of Civil Engineering, FISAT, Dr. Jiji Antony (HOD), Dr. Kavitha P E (Assoc. Prof.), Mr. Pauly P K(Technical Staff) visited the university at Pahang on 7/12/2023 and 8/12/2023 for the exchange of MoU, to experience the research and laboratory facilities available and to have a technical discussion on the possible areas of collaboration.

    The team from FISAT also visited the lab facilities of the university. The civil engineering related UG/PG labs and the central lab facilities were visited.
