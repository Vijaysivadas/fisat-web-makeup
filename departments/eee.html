    About the department

    Electrical and Electronics Engineering

    60B.Tech seats · Electrical & Electronics Engineering
    12M.Tech seats · Power Electronics and Power Systems
    14Faculty members
    2002Established

    The Department of Electrical And Electronics Engineering was established in the year 2002 with a primary objective of offering value-added educational experience through quality teaching that prepares graduates for a range of career opportunities in today’s world. The Department offers B.Tech in Electrical and Electronics Engineering , an NBA accredited programme with a sanctioned intake of 60 students. The Post Graduate Programme in Power Electronics and Power Systems was started in the year 2011 with an annual intake of 12. Department is also recognized by APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University as a research center for Ph.D programmes. The department is fortified by a team of highly motivated, dedicated and experienced teachers graduated from institutions of good repute and is well equipped with the latest hardware and software lab facilities.

    Purpose

    Vision & Mission

    Vision

    To equip the students to meet evolving technological demands through quality education and research, shaping them into socially responsible, globally competent and ethical engineers who contribute to sustainable nation building and human progress.

    Mission 1

    To deliver quality education that nurtures critical thinking and lifelong learning, empowering students to excel as competent engineers in a dynamic technological world

    Mission 2

    To promote research and innovation that advance competencies and enable learners to create sustainable solutions to global challenges in Electrical and Electronics Engineering.

    Mission 3

    To foster ethical and socially responsible professionals, who drive nation building and human progress through technological advancements.

    Programmes

    B.Tech

    B.Tech in Electrical and Electronics Engineering.

    Electrical and Electronics Engineering is one of the core Engineering branches started at Federal Institute of Science and Technology in the year 2002 with an undergraduate program (B.Tech) affiliated to M. G. University, Kottayam, Kerala with a sanctioned strength of 60 students. Since 2015 admission onwards, B.Tech degree program is affiliated to APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU).Our Department has many university rank holders in B.Tech as well as M.Tech Programmes.The Department has excellent placement records in companies like Infosys, TCS, UST Global, Tata Elxsi.Our students has got opportunities to do internships in various companies during their course of study.

    The Department has excellent lab facilities with modern software tools and a research lab for facilitating research and project works of students.Teaching aids like projectors, smart boards are made use to make the teaching learning process effective.In addition to this teachers use facilities like Moodle,Edx and several online platforms to improve the quality of course delivery .

    ‘Learn From Great Minds’ lecture series is conducted every year with eminent resource persons who share their industrial experiences.Subject related events like seminars, industrial visits, etc are organized to enhance the subject knowledge.Alumni Webinar series is conducted with our old students as subject experts which facilitates the interaction between students and Alumni.Several add on courses are conducted aiming at academic enrichment , skill development and career orientation of students.Placement training programmes and mock interviews are arranged which enables the students to attend placement drive confidently.Academic-industrial interactions are created by organizing, industrial visits, in -plant training and regular soft skill development training programmes. Students are encouraged to undergo industrial training of at least one week during the semester holidays.

    M.Tech

    M.Tech in Power Electronics and Power Systems

    Power System had been a thrust area in Electrical Engineering. But with the advent of Power Electronics devices, the contemporary Power System had changed and it took a new phase. Power Electronics has significant role in various phases of Power System. The generation of electric power using alternative/ renewable energy sources has become very popular nowadays. With the intention of giving the students to explore deep into the trending areas, the Post Graduate Programme in Power Electronics and Power Systems was started in the year 2011 with an annual intake of 12.

    Facilities

    Laboratories

    The department has a well adequate lab facility with all equipment needed to meet the curriculum requirements.There are 6 laboratories functioning under the department. Every laboratory has an adequate number of qualified technical staff. To enhance the overall learning experience, the department library has a collection of important books, and it is equipped with computers with internet facility, which makes the library a self learning centre.

    Electrical Engineering Workshop

    Electrical Engineering Workshop practice is the backbone of the real industrial environment which helps to develop and enhance relevant technical hand skills required by the technician working in the various engineering industries and workshops. Workshop curriculum builds hands-on experiences which would help to learn wiring practices and technology courses in successive semesters.

    Circuits and Measurements Lab

    The Circuits & Measurements Lab is the one of the basic Laboratory of Electrical and Electronics Engineering Department. The Laboratory is equipped with conventional and modern Electrical and Electronics measuring equipment, various AC and DC Electrical meters Transformers. This Laboratory helps Under Graduate Students to get acquainted with the basics of measurements and selection of proper measuring instruments. Also, the Laboratory is used for research activities and to carry out project works.

    Electrical Machines Lab

    The Electrical Machines Laboratory is the one of the important Laboratory of Electrical and Electronics Engineering Department. The Laboratory is equipped with conventional and modern Electrical and Electronics measuring equipments, various AC and DC Electrical Machines and Transformers. This Laboratory caters the need of Under Graduate Students to enable them to easily understand the basics and adapt to advanced experiments of Electrical Machines. Also, the Laboratory is used for research activities in Electrical drives and to carry out project works

    Advanced Electrical Simulation Lab

    Advanced Electrical Simulation Lab comprises of various advanced software which are used in evaluating the basic and advanced level problems in Electrical Engineering. This Lab serves the need of both under graduate and post graduate students enabling them to easily understand the basics and advanced concepts. Advanced simulation projects are carried out using MATLAB tool box and PSCAD. It also open-ups the diverse opportunities for conducting research work for both faculty and students in the domains of Power Systems, Power Electronics and Drives, Control Systems etc.

    Power Electronics Lab

    The Power Electronics Laboratory enhances the knowledge of Undergraduate Students and Post Graduate Students by providing better understanding of the concepts and working of advanced Power Semiconductor Devices , Power Electronics Circuits and electric drives. Power Electronics lab provides fundamental facilities for the students to get an overview of different types of power semiconductor devices and the operation and characteristics of various types of power electronic converters. This Laboratory is equipped with Single Phase and Three Phase AC to DC Converters, different types of DC-DC Choppers, Single Phase AC Voltage Controllers, Cyclo-converters, Single Phase and Three Phase Inverters ,Induction motor drives,Dc motor drives etc. In addition to that the Post Graduate Students and Research Scholars utilize this Laboratory to carry out their project and research work respectively.

    Centre for Advanced Research in Power Converters – CARPC(Project Lab)

    The Center for Advanced Research in Power Converters started with a big dream, the dream of excellence. It aims in achieving excellence through industrial collaborations, thereby sharing the technical knowledge and providing transformative industry standard solutions to many of the benchmark problems. The center exists in motivating students and researchers of the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering to pursue research in Power Electronics and related areas. It plans to provide training in the field of power electronics, give technical assistance and facilities for students for their project work, and also conduct Faculty Development Programs. The thrust areas include development of power management modules for various applications ranging from battery chargers to automated vehicles, development of high efficiency Power Converters, methods for Optimal Renewable Energy Management.

    Scope of employment

    An Electrical and Electronics Engineer has a wide range of job opportunities both as an Electrical Engineer and Electronics Engineer in various Govt. Sectors, Industries and PSUs and as a software Engineer in IT sectors. Electrical Engineers are an inevitable part of all industries and can work in domains like construction, engineering works, electrical system design, development, testing, control, programming ,automation, project planning and realization. Opportunities are expected to increase in the trending areas of renewable energy and hybrid vehicles in near future. Battery management systems, charging infrastructure, Motor Drives and Power Electronics – these are all EEE domains. Every EV on an Indian road represents a new job for an Electrical Engineer. Industry 4.0 – the fusion of automation, IoT, AI, and robotics with traditional engineering is led by Electrical and Electronics Engineers. PLC/SCADA automation, smart metering, drone systems, and industrial robotics also belong to the EEE domain. The widest career canvas include Power Generation, Transmission, Distribution, Smart Grids ,Solar/Wind Energy in core power and EV & Battery Tech, Health Care technologies, robotics & IoT , Automation in emerging technologies. The other career areas include Embedded Systems, Control Software, Energy Analytics and Semiconductors.

    PSU Opportunities
