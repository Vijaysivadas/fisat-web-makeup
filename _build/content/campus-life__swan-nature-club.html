SWAN (Sanguine Workers and Admirers of Nature) – Nature Club of FISAT

The Nature Club of FISAT was established in the year 2002. The nature club SWAN aims at creating a new generation of youth, which recognizes the importance of preserving nature and creates eco friendly and green solutions. Its main objective is to give awareness to the students regarding habitat preservation, planting and maintaining nature garden. The club frequently arranges fieldtrips; nature walks and also arranges talks by naturalists.
