IEEE FISAT SB [Federal Institute of Science and Technology Student Branch] is a technical organization under India Council in Kerala Chapter that has actively volunteered and conducted events to benefit Engineering students and enthusiasts.

The Student Branch of FISAT was formed in 2003 with the motto, “Integrating Innovations” aimed at activities ranging from technical to managerial adventure to entertainment.

Shri. S Gopakumar, the Student’s Activities Chairman, and IEEE GOLD MEMBER Shri. Vargheese Cherian took the initial steps to plant the roots of the World’s largest technical and professional organization at FISAT.

IEEE FISAT Student Chapter has been rated among the top 5 active student branches in the Kerala section. There is a total count of 450+ members who are very active and explore the different possibilities in the world.

The Student Branch has an active PES, RAS and CS, ComSoc chapters, and WIE affinity group. Sustained volunteering in a variety of events in and around Student Branches, speaking in and attending chapter sessions and workshops, our members explore new realms of interest and adapt to different situations enabling them to easily fit into any technical or professional role, even after their college life.
