The Campus is at Hormis Nagar, Mookkannoor, a rustic village on the outskirts of Angamaly Town. A vast stretch of land comprising nearly 40 acres forms the campus. Mookkannoor being the birthplace of Late K. P. Hormis, the great visionary and founder of Federal Bank, holds emotional bondage with each and every member of the Federal family and its well-wishers. Therefore the campus is named Hormis Nagar. With its scenic beauty and virgin surroundings, this typical Kerala Village is expected to provide proper ambiance for the prestigious academic institution. The campus is within a radius of hardly 4 km from NH-544, 7 km from Angamaly Railway station and 11 km.from Kochi International Airport. Well-known pilgrim centers like Kalady, the birthplace of Adi Sankara, and Malayattoor, blessed with the footprints of St. Thomas and the famous Athirappily Waterfalls, one of the important tourist attractions of the state are also in the vicinity of the campus.

How to reach the Campus

The FISAT campus is very near to Mookkannoor, a small township on the border of Angamaly Municipal Town. It is well connected by road to Angamaly from where a number of private and K.S.R.T.C buses make frequent trips to Mookkannoor and destinations beyond Mookkannoor. Mookkannoor is on the bus route from Angamaly to Athirappilly Waterfalls on the south bank of the Chalakudy River. It is also connected by bus service to Kalady via Thuravoor and to Chalakudy via Azhakom.
