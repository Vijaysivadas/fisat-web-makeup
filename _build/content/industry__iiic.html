Industry Institute Interaction Cell (IIIC) – FISAT

The Industry Institute Interaction Cell (IIIC) of Federal Institute of Science and Technology serves as a dynamic platform that bridges the gap between academia and industry. Established with the vision of fostering meaningful collaboration between students, faculty, and industrial partners, IIIC plays a pivotal role in enhancing industry relevance in technical education.

Vision

To create a strong and sustainable industry–academia ecosystem that fosters innovation, skill development, technology transfer, industry readiness, and global professional competence.

Mission

  • To build sustained industry linkages that enable knowledge exchange, skill enhancement, and practical exposure for students and faculty.
  • To provide students with exposure to real-world industrial practices and emerging technologies.
  • To promote internships, industrial visits, expert talks, and skill development programs.
  • To encourage applied research, sponsored projects, and technology transfer initiatives.

Objectives

  • Strengthen academia–industry partnerships.
  • Enhance student employability through practical training and certification programs.
  • Support faculty development through industrial exposure and consultancy opportunities.
  • Promote innovation, entrepreneurship, and start-up culture within the institution.

Key Activities

 Organizing industrial visits and in-plant training programs to provide real-time exposure to industrial practices.

 Conducting expert lectures, conclaves, workshops, and seminars by industry professionals on emerging technologies and industry trends.

 Facilitating Faculty–Industry Interaction Programmes to enhance professional collaboration and knowledge exchange.

 Signing Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs) with reputed industries and organizations for strategic academic and technical partnerships.

 Promoting internships, live projects, certification programs, and skill development initiatives to enhance industry readiness and employability.

Through its sustained efforts, IIIC at FISAT ensures that students are industry-ready, technically competent, and professionally confident. By integrating academic knowledge with industrial expertise, the cell contributes significantly to the holistic development of students and the advancement of the institution’s academic excellence, innovation ecosystem, and professional reputation.

The Industry Institute Interaction Cell (IIIC) of (FISAT) serves as a dynamic platform that bridges the gap between academia and industry. The Cell is committed to enhancing industry collaboration, promoting experiential learning, and preparing students to meet the evolving demands of the global workforce. IIIC functions as a strategic interface between students, faculty, and leading industries by facilitating meaningful partnerships, technical interactions, and collaborative initiatives. It plays a pivotal role in strengthening industry readiness among students through structured programs and industry-aligned activities. The Cell facilitates industrial visit and in-plant training, expert talks, conclaves, workshops, seminars, skill development programs, faculty development initiatives to ensure practical exposure alongside academic learning.
