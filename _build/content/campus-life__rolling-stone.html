Rolling Stone- Literary Association of FISAT is a platform which showcases the literary and creative talents of students. It is a venue where their soft skills are pruned in order to develop their overall personality. Competitions are held in various literary items (Elocution, debate, essay writing, poetry writing, painting, poster designing, film review, and the like) to pick out the best to represent FISAT at Inter-collegiate competitions. Moreover students organize various functions through which they get training in Event Management. The Rolling Stone was formally inaugurated on Wednesday 28th September 2011 by Dr. Muse Mary George (Writer, Critic). The Rolling Stone currently functions under Mr. Thomas Philip (Assistant Professor, Science and Humanities Department).
