Inclusiveness

The institution promotes an inclusive attitude. FISAT has a policy of equal access to opportunities and resources for all people, regardless of gender, caste, creed, community, or religion. The gender equity cell organises a variety of educational programmes to ensure that everyone has equal access to opportunities. Different religious festivals are celebrated.

Nurturing

FISAT has a policy of encouraging students in their all round development. Students are nurtured by their teachers and mentors to develop their talents, skills and attitude to develop into competent individuals ready to take up the challenges of the real world

Social Commitment

FISAT organizes various activities to reach out to the community and provide support to the under privileged sections of the society. As part of its social commitment, many students are provided merit cum means scholarships.

Professionalism

FISAT believes in consistently achieving high standards, both visibly and “behind the scenes” – whatever be the role – student or staff.

Integrity

FISAT promotes the practice of being honest and showing a consistent and uncompromising adherence to strong moral and ethical principles and values.

Respect

FISAT believes in giving due regard for the feelings, wishes, or rights of others.

Excellence

True to its motto of “Focus on Excellence”, FISAT strives to achieve excellence in all its endeavors.
