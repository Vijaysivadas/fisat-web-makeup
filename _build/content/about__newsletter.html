The FISATian Herald

The Newsletter “The FISATian Herald" is a rich treasure of opportunities received, experiences learned, and accolades achieved through the teamwork and determination of FISATians. The Editorial Board consists of members (one member each) from all departments. This is a historical document of all achievements (of students and faculty members) and milestones of the college. In addition, the visits by eminent personalities during major functions and events are recorded. Truly “The FISATian Herald" is a valuable and precious archive of all documents of the college.
