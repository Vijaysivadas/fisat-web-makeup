Centre for Future Skills (CFS) at FISAT

Empowering Innovation Through Experiential Learning

The National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC), under the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE), Government of India, plays a pivotal role in advancing skill-based education and enhancing employability across sectors. In alignment with this national mission, the Federal Institute of Science and Technology (FISAT), in association with Ethnotech Academy, Bengaluru—an implementing partner of NSDC—has established the Centre for Future Skills (CFS), a transformative initiative aimed at equipping students with industry-relevant, globally recognized skills alongside their academic education. The Centre for Future Skills represents a significant step toward bridging the gap between classroom learning and industry expectations. Designed as a technology-enabled, experiential learning ecosystem, CFS provides students with opportunities to gain practical expertise in emerging technologies that are shaping the future workforce. With strong industry collaborations and advanced infrastructure, these labs enable a “learn by doing” approach that fosters innovation, creativity, and problem-solving skills among students.

Industry Partners

The CFS is strengthened by a robust network of industry partners, including global leaders such as Apple, Microsoft, AWS, Autodesk, Intel, IBM, Schneider Electric, and Intel, along with specialized partners like Ansys, Cisco, Unity etc. The Ethnotech Academy conducts training for global certification programs, providing access to industry-standard tools, certified courses, and real-world exposure that enhance the learning experience.

Key Highlights of CFS
  • Pioneering Initiative: First engineering college in Kerala to establish a Centre for Future Skills
  • Industry-Integrated Infrastructure: Advanced labs powered by global leaders
  • Hands-on Experiential Learning: Strong emphasis on real-world, practical learning
  • Globally Recognized Certifications: Credentials aligned with international standards
  • 120+ Industry-Relevant Courses: Seamlessly integrated into academic curriculum
  • Multidisciplinary Ecosystem: Exposure to AI, Cloud, IoT, VLSI, AR/VR, and more
  • Structured training, mentorship, and portfolio development

Objectives of CFS

  • Bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements
  • Equip students for global, future-ready career opportunities
  • Provide access to NSDC-certified programs enhancing employability
  • Foster a culture of continuous upskilling and reskilling


State-of-the-Art Lab Ecosystem

CFS at FISAT houses seven specialized global laboratories that provide hands-on training in emerging technologies while fostering innovation, creativity, and industry-relevant skills. Through its advanced infrastructure, industry partnerships, and future-focused learning ecosystem, CFS has become a vibrant hub of skill development, empowering students to evolve into competent professionals, technology leaders, and future-ready innovators.
