The FISAT Medical Centre provides comprehensive healthcare services to students and staff, supporting both physical and mental well-being. The centre includes:

  • Doctor’s Consultation Room: A highly qualified and experienced medical doctor is available Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM – 12:30 PM, providing primary healthcare, medical consultation, first aid, and referrals when necessary.
  • Student Counseling Room: A full-time qualified Mental Health Counsellor (Psychologist) offers confidential psychological counselling and emotional support to students.
  • Observation Beds: Four beds are available for students requiring short-term rest, observation, or first-aid assistance before further medical care, if needed.
