The AICTE Idea Lab aims to boost innovation, creativity, and hands-on learning for engineering students by providing resources to turn theory into practical solutions and prototypes.

Functions: Idea Labs promote hands-on learning by encouraging experimentation and troubleshooting. They foster innovation, creativity, and entrepreneurial skills by supporting idea brainstorming, design, and product development. The labs facilitate interdisciplinary collaboration and provide access to modern tools like 3D printers and robotics. Mentorship from faculty and industry experts guides projects. The labs showcase student work, leading to recognition and further opportunities, ultimately enhancing employability through practical experience.

Importance: Idea Labs bridge the industry-academia gap by focusing on practical, industry-relevant skills. They cultivate a culture of innovation, address societal challenges through student-developed solutions, and boost research and development. By emphasizing hands-on creation over rote learning, they improve the quality of engineering education. They also act as incubators for student start-ups, contributing to global competitiveness and fostering essential soft skills like critical thinking and teamwork for personal and professional growth.

Proposed activities of AICTE IDEA Lab The proposed AICTE IDEA Lab activities focus on fostering innovation, practical skills, and an entrepreneurial mindset among students and faculty. Key activities include:

  1. Hands-on Training and Workshops on modern tools (3D printing, robotics, IoT) to ensure proficiency in lab equipment.
  2. Idea Generation through pitching and brainstorming sessions to encourage interdisciplinary collaboration.
  3. Project Incubation and Mentorship to help transform concepts into functional prototypes with expert guidance.
  4. Competitions to promote collaborative problem-solving skills for real-world challenges.
  5. Open Lab Sessions for independent experimentation and self-directed learning.
  6. Industry Collaboration (sponsorships, internships) to bridge the industry-academia gap.
  7. Outreach Programs to promote STEM and inspire the local community.
  8. Entrepreneurship Development Programs to support the development of student start-ups.
  9. Showcase Events to recognize achievements and create funding opportunities.
  10. Faculty Development Programs to ensure sustainable and high-quality mentorship.
AICTE IDEA Lab Financial Support The AICTE IDEA Lab scheme provides one-third financial assistance to institutions for the establishment and upkeep of innovation centers. The total allocated sum for the scheme is ninety lakh rupees, of which thirty lakhs will be contributed by AICTE. An additional thirty lakhs is required to be secured from industries or alumni. The FISAT Alumni association generously contributed thirty lakh rupees towards this initiative.Additionally FISAT Management has contributed an amount of 50 lakhs for furnishing the lab.

FISAT AICTE IDEA LAb The FISAT AICTE IDEA Lab, sanctioned in February 2025 and funded in May 2025, has completed major equipment procurement, including a PCB milling machine, four 3D printers, a laser cutter, a wood router, and fully equipped workbenches . The necessary ideation, prototyping, and tutorial rooms were furnished by August 2025. Programmatic activities began on August 18, 2025, as per AICTE schedule.

Within the three months succeeding this commencement, we have successfully conducted several key programs, specifically Upskilling programs, Maker Hours (an IDEA Lab familiarization program), and Launch Pad (a program introducing first-year students to the lab).

Additionally, the FISAT AICTE IDEA Lab organized a three-day rehabilitation program for mentally challenged individuals residing in the Mookkannoor area. The IDEA Lab faculty team identified five beneficiaries from the Buds school within Mookkannoor Panchayath for this event, which took place from December 3rd to December 5th. The primary objectives of this initiative were to empower participants with practical skills in design and prototyping, to introduce entrepreneurial opportunities conducive to self-reliance, and to provide hands-on exposure to modern tools and technologies. The event was conducted successfully, and follow-up actions to support the Buds school in obtaining grants for establishing a dedicated rehabilitation center are currently in progress.

The FISAT AICTE IDEA Lab also conducted the "Science Safari" program for the benefit of school students from Vidyodaya and Ideal Public School in Aluva. A total of 132 students participated in and benefited from this program, which was held from February 3rd to February 6th, 2026.

Our Lab actively supports student projects, and we are pleased to report a notable achievement: a project developed entirely within the IDEA Lab secured the third prize in the All India competition of ASME EFx India 2026, held in Jaipur. Furthermore, twelve other projects are currently under development within the IDEA Lab. The Lab remains committed to fostering student innovation by offering comprehensive support, encompassing technical guidance, fabrication assistance, and financial provision for the acquisition of project-specific consumables.
