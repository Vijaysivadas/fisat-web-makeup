    FISAT places a high premium on the safe transportation of students and personnel to and from the institution. To provide safe transportation, the college has its own bus fleet. With a fleet of 25 buses plying various routes, FISAT has an outstanding transportation infrastructure in place, providing easy access even from the most remote regions of the district. On all working days, day scholar students have access to the bus service. Hostel students can also board the college buses for going home on weekends and holidays by availing the bus coupon facility

    Our fleet of 30 buses covers a 50-kilometer radius in all directions.
