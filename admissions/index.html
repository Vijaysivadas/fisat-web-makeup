    Department Of Computer Applications

    The MCA programme started in Federal Institute of Science and Technology (FISAT) in the year 2006. The department aims to achieve academic excellence by focusing on the key activities like providing quality education to students, enhancing their learning skills and giving them training to acquire the professional skills required for their career with an objective of making them socially committed professionals. A team of qualified and committed faculty with ample experience is the highlight of the department.

    The students of the department consistently bagged top spots in the University Examinations. The first ranks in 2010, 2012 and 2015, second rank in 2015 and third rank in 2011, 2016 (MCA-Lateral) in Mahatma Gandhi University MCA examinations and third rank in 2020 in APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University were major academic achievements of the department. Regular industrial visits and personality development trainings have been organizing by the department to improve the employability. Reputed MNCs and companies have recruited many of our graduates in their campus placement drives. Department has signed MOU with companies for providing internship, training and project works. ​The department constantly strives to improve the knowledge and skill sets of the students by organizing many values added programs like workshops, webinars, project exhibitions, add-on courses etc.

    Courses Offered:

    MCA (Two Year)

    • Two year Post graduate programme consisting of four semesters.
    • The annual intake is 60.
    • The course is approved by AICTE and affiliated to the A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technological University, Kerala.

    Eligibility

    • Passed any graduation degree (e.g.: B.E. / B.Tech./ B.Sc / B.Com. / B.A./ B. Voc./ BCA etc.,) preferably with Mathematics at 10+2 level or at Graduation level
    • Obtained at least 50% marks (45% marks in case of candidates belonging to reserved category) inthe qualifying examination.
    • Must qualify entrance examination conducted by a competent authority as per the rules and regulations of APJ/AICTE/Govt.

    MBA Admissions 2026

    Apply Now

    FISAT Business School invites applications for admission to its two year Full Time MBA Program – Batch 2026-28

    Total intake: 120 seats

    Eligibility

    A pass in degree examination with 50% marks (60% marks desirable) in aggregate for Arts/Commerce /Science/ Engineering and for post graduates. Final year students are also eligible to apply.

    Good score in any Management Aptitude Test- C-MAT / CAT/KMAT (The minimum qualifying cut off marks of KMAT Kerala Entrance Examination shall be 15% (108/720) for General Category from the academic year 2019. The minimum cut off for SEBC will be 10% (72/720) and 7.5% (54/720) for SC/ST).

    Selection

    The selection of candidates is purely based on merit. Admission to the MBA programme is determined by the scores obtained by the candidate in the admission test, group discussion, personal interview and a consistent academic record .

    How to Apply

    You can apply online from our website, www.fbs.ac.in or www.fisat.ac.in

    Application Fee: Rs.750

    Note: Fees are subjected to changes by Government/University Orders, time to time

    Note: The Candidates have to pay the fees after getting confirmation from college for admission.

    Fees can be paid either by Draft favouring FISAT payable at Ernakulam/Aluva or by Cash or by cheque or by swiping ATM/Debit card or by transfer to FISAT Bank Account No: 17140100001036 at Federal Bank Hormis Nagar Branch (IFSC: FDRL0001714).
